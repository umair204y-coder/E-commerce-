Built by people who were tired of bad gear

VELORA started in 2021 with a simple frustration: most "tech accessories" sold online were either overpriced imports or unreliable knockoffs. We began sourcing and testing gear ourselves — keeping only what survived a year of daily use — and built VELORA around that filter.

Today we ship across Pakistan from our Lahore warehouse, working directly with a small set of manufacturers so we can guarantee quality without the retail markup of a middleman.

What we hold ourselves to

Tested, not just listed

Every product on this site has been used by our team for at least two weeks before it goes live.

Honest shipping times

The delivery estimate you see at checkout is the one our warehouse actually hits, not an optimistic guess.

A person answers

Every contact form and message reaches an actual support agent — no bot loops.
