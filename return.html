Return & Refund Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Our 7-day promise

If something isn't right, you can return most items within 7 days of delivery for a full refund or exchange. We keep this process simple on purpose — no long forms, no runaround.

What qualifies for a return

  • Item is unused, unworn, and in its original condition
  • Original packaging, tags, and accessories are included
  • Proof of purchase (order number or receipt) is provided
  • Request is made within 7 days of the delivery date

What doesn't qualify

  • Items marked "Final Sale" at checkout
  • Products showing signs of use, damage, or missing components not caused by us
  • Earbuds and other hygiene-sensitive items once the seal is opened, unless defective
  • Returns requested after the 7-day window without a manufacturing defect

Damaged, defective, or wrong items

If your order arrives damaged, faulty, or different from what you ordered, contact us within 48 hours with photos of the item and its packaging. We'll cover the return shipping and send a replacement or full refund at no extra cost.

How to start a return

  1. Message us via the contact page or email support@velora.pk with your order number and reason for return.
  2. Our team confirms eligibility and arranges a free pickup from your address (or shares a drop-off point in select cities).
  3. Once the item passes inspection at our warehouse, we process your refund or dispatch your exchange.

Refund timelines

Refunds are issued within 5–7 business days of us receiving and inspecting the returned item. Card payments are refunded to the original card; cash-on-delivery orders are refunded via bank transfer or store credit, whichever you prefer.

Exchanges

Want a different size or color instead of a refund? Let us know when you request the return — we'll ship the replacement as soon as the original item is picked up, subject to stock availability.

Questions?

Reach out any time at support@velora.pk or 0300-1234567. We're happy to help sort things out.
